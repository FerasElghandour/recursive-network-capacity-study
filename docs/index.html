How it works

Fixed splits → Matched models → Ten paired seeds → Held-out metrics

The full study records 960 main runs and 160 corrected-compute runs. This explorer shows the native feature-order results, ten seeds per cell. Seed SD measures variability; it is not a confidence interval.

Balanced models exceed random-tree mean accuracy in 14 of 16 native cells, and the corrected-compute dense comparator in 5 of 16. Dense models remain faster. Four representative runs were freshly reproduced.

These finite tabular experiments do not establish a universal architectural advantage.
